# Velká a malá detektivka – series plan (16 issues × 16 pages)
The issues run from autumn to the end of next summer. A running mystery connects them: děda's childhood club, "Klub Hvězdička".
Nobody is ever the "bad guy". The false lead changes each time, and so does who finds the key clue.
Before writing each issue, ask the family for real details (names, places, recent events).

1. Záhada zmizelých ponožek (autumn) – DONE (comic)
2. Tajemství starého klíče – DONE (comic, 20 pages): attic noises are a barn owl with three owlets; děda's Klub Hvězdička flashback
   (friends Franta and Věrka; Věrka moved away and hid her half of the map with a riddle); Alica reads the riddle, Hanka finds the half
3. Mapa ke starému rybníku – DONE (comic, 24 pages): the treasure was already dug up; suspects Tonda, the badger, the fisherman;
   the culprit is kind Věrka, back as the neighbour at the mill, who saved it from the diggers. Treasure: a silver tolar,
   her father's brass spyglass, a club photo, a spare badge (now Tonda's) and Franta's picture letter; the map is printed inside
4. Hanka to vyřeší! – DONE (comic, 24 pages): Alica is sick in bed and runs HQ by walkie-talkie; Hanka reads Franta's picture letter
   (old henhouse → church clock stopped at three, its hand points → the "Bota" rock → a star in the crack) and finds his tin.
   Someone walks the trail ahead (zigzag boot prints); suspects the sexton, a magpie, the fisherman. The fisherman is Franta:
   he left a new picture letter. Little Franta took the clock's winding key, so the clock stood at three for 60 years; it runs again.
   Hidden-object game: 9 hearts
5. Kdo staví sněhuláky? – DONE (comic, 24 pages, first in the personal style): snowmen appear every morning; they are picture
   letters from shy Bětka, Franta's granddaughter staying for the winter (parsnip noses, star-soled boots, red pompom).
   False leads Tonda and babička (baking gingerbread → issue 6). Alica reads the snowmen, Hanka answers with snowmen.
   Bětka joins Klub Hvězdička "na zkoušku". Hidden-object game: 10 triangles with a dot
6. Kam mizí perníčky? – DONE (comic, 24 pages): two thieves on two trails. Tits peck the broken pieces on the sill (solved
   on p. 8, feeder built), but whole iced stars keep vanishing from the tin in the locked house: děda revives the club's
   Advent stars on the spruce by the mill (7 stars = 7 members, then 2 more because the tits ate the girls' first ones).
   Wrong leads: mice (děda's unbaited trap), Joey (floury paws), Hanka (dark chocolate crumbs vs white-iced stars).
   Hidden-object game: 12 gingerbread men (checked with art/check_hidden.py)
7. Joeyho velký den – told by Joey; he finds babička's ring in the snow
8. Stopy ve sněhu – DONE (comic, 24 pages): parsnip noses vanish from the snowmen; the hajný teaches tracks (full-page
   guide: deer, boar, hare, fox, dog, cat, squirrel, bird). False leads Joey, hare, fox, boar. Culprit a roe doe: noses too high
   for a hare, frayed bite (no upper front teeth), split print without dewclaw dots on snow děda smoothed. Ending: veg for the
   krmelec, pinecone noses. Hidden-object game: 10 carrots
9. Tajná zpráva v knihovně – DONE (comic, 28 pages): a slip in hvězdičkové písmo (the club's star code: shape + dice dots)
   falls out of a library book. Culprit babička (Jana): 60 years ago děda (Honza) asked her to Masopust in that code
   ("Budu medvěd") and she cracked it alone. Clues: pinking-shear zigzag edge, lavender (issue 7), she reads a letter
   without the key, the star drawn in one stroke, she picked the book. False leads Tonda, Franta, Věrka (a star behind
   her back), děda. Parade STOP page; the girls are an owl and a bear cub. Bedtime issue: no pencil needed.
   Hidden-object game: 10 koblížky
10. Záhada ve škole – DONE (comic, 28 pages): the forest school (two yurts + a log building with the kumbál); guinea pigs
   Míša and Šiša vanish from the hutch. Hay trail = a hedgehog building its nest (nature page). Culprit pan Karel the školník
   (kind): broken latch + frost, he moved them to the warm kumbál into his woolly hat; his note "… v teple. K." blew into the
   nest. False leads the hedgehog, malý Karel ("K.", hay from his rabbit), táta (wood shavings, a box with air holes = new
   hutch parts + hay). Hanka's key clue: a rustling bag makes them wheek. Field day builds a new hutch. First issue with
   máma and táta. Bedtime issue: no pencil. Hidden-object game: 10 crayons
11. Kam mizí vajíčka? – DONE (comic, 28 pages): Easter at the chalupa; babička's 8 hens lay one egg short every day.
   Culprit Kropenatka, the grey broody hen with lots of white freckles, sneaking through a gap under the run's wire to a secret nest behind the straw
   bales in the barn (Hanka finds her by torch, the only one small enough). Clues: no shells anywhere, straw in her feathers
   (the henhouse uses táta's wood shavings), a speckled feather and hen prints at the gap, she clucks and puffs up, Joey pulls
   to the barn. False leads fox (Joey's prints), magpie (foil, ribbon and the issue-1 teaspoon), Tonda (he brings eggs),
   hedgehog. No rooster, so no chicks: at the Easter Monday koleda Věrka gives eggs from under her rooster Pepík; the found
   eggs become kraslice; chicks three weeks later. Dyeing page (onion skins, wax, markers) and a broody-hen page.
   Bedtime issue: no pencil. Hidden-object game: 10 kraslice
12. Tajemné šipky – DONE (comic, 28 pages): the week before Alica's 8th birthday chalk arrows appear at the chalupa and
   at home in the village, and everyone acts strangely. Alica investigates her own surprise party; Hanka is secretly in
   on it (the first time the sisters are on opposite sides). The three arrow styles are the main deduction (táta: long and
   solid, high on the fence, oil smudges; máma: dashed with a heart, like her lunchbox notes; Hanka: small dotted ones
   near the ground). False leads: Tonda (a real hopscotch, invitations in his pocket), babička (flour, the cake), děda
   (a "magpie" mewing in the locked shed = Micka's kittens), táta (wide knobbly tyre tracks to the garage = the new pink
   bike with a front fork), máma (the torn list "balónky, limo, 8 sv…"), Věrka and Bětka (odd questions, the banner).
   The trail on the birthday: under the stairs (Hanka's torch, a star that says TAM), the hopscotch (SKOČ), Joey sitting
   on an arrow, the garden gate, then a garden party with everybody. Presents: Hanka's cat drawing and the stick she
   peeled at the forest kindergarten, děda's brown kitten (malá Micka, old Micka's daughter), the pink bike. Arrow-guide
   page, arrow maze and spot-the-difference activity. Bedtime issue: no pencil. Hidden-object game: 10 presents.
   Hanka's party comes in a later issue
13. Stopy u potoka – done (28 pages, bedtime). Klub Hvězdička (with Bětka and děda) camps on Hvězdná louka by the
   Lišní potok, the meadow where the old Klub camped. Willows fall at night (pencil stumps, chips), děda's walking stick
   disappears and the stepping stones go under. The culprit is Hryzálek, a young beaver building his first dam. False
   leads: táta's saw (flat cuts), Hanka's knife (long curls), Joey (tooth holes), Franta's "vydra" (small round prints),
   Tonda's werewolf. Bětka draws the measuring rock every day, which proves the water is rising. Alica's lupa finds the
   two-teeth grooves, and she follows the trail upstream on her new bike. The hajný explains, and there's a beaver fact
   page. The tail slap soaks Joey. Mesh goes round the camp willows. Below the dam an old Klub tin turns up: the badge,
   a photo, and a castle drawing marked XIV ("Další poklad je na…", torn off). Activity: animals ↔ homes and spot the
   differences. Hidden-object game: 10 frogs
14. Poklad na hradě – a castle trip
15. Noc padajících hvězd – the Perseids at an observatory
16. Klub Hvězdička – the finale; the girls get star badges
