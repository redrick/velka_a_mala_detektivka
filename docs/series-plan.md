# Velká a malá detektivka – series plan (16 issues × 16 pages)
The issues run from autumn to the end of next summer. A running mystery connects them: děda's childhood club, "Klub Hvězdička".
Nobody is ever the "bad guy". The false lead changes each time, and so does who finds the key clue.
Before writing each issue, ask the family for real details (names, places, recent events).

1. Záhada zmizelých ponožek (autumn) – DONE (comic)
2. Tajemství starého klíče – DONE (comic, 20 pages): attic noises are a barn owl with three owlets; děda's Klub Hvězdička flashback
   (friends Franta and Věrka; Věrka moved away and hid her half of the map with a riddle); Alica reads the riddle, Hanka finds the half
3. Mapa ke starému rybníku – a treasure hunt; the map is printed inside the comic
4. Hanka to vyřeší! – Alica is sick in bed; Hanka and Joey lead the case
5. Kdo staví sněhuláky? – clues hidden in the pictures; the answer is a shy new friend
6. Kam mizí perníčky? – Advent; hungry tits (sýkorky); the girls build a bird feeder
7. Joeyho velký den – told by Joey; he finds babička's ring in the snow
8. Stopy ve sněhu – the forester; a guide to animal tracks
9. Tajná zpráva v knihovně – Masopust; a cipher to crack
10. Záhada ve škole – Alica's class hamster; Mum and Dad appear
11. Kam mizí vajíčka? – Easter; a hen hides her eggs
12. Tajemné šipky – everyone seems suspicious; the arrows lead to a surprise party for Hanka
13. Stopy u potoka – camping; a young beaver
14. Poklad na hradě – a castle trip
15. Noc padajících hvězd – the Perseids at an observatory
16. Klub Hvězdička – the finale; the girls get star badges
