Comics and a point-and-click game, made at home for two small detectives

What is this?

A family project for two sisters, a big one (7) and a little one (4). They solve gentle mysteries at their grandparents' cottage, with a magnifier, a torch, a plush orangutan and a very clever border collie called Joey.

Every story is first a printed comic, read at bedtime. The next day the girls play the same story on the computer, where it gets new puzzles the comic doesn't have. The goal is to get small kids comfortable on a computer through play, and to practise logic and reading on the way.

16 storiesOne series, from autumn to the next summer, held together by grandpa's childhood club, Klub Hvězdička.
14 readyIssues 1–14 exist as printable comics and as playable game stories.
Drawn in codeEvery picture is drawn in Python, the same library for the comic and the game.

Made for small hands

  • One click does the obvious thing. No verb menus, big targets.
  • Nothing scary, no way to lose, no timers. Every "culprit" is an animal or a kind person.
  • Stuck? Click the dog. Joey sniffs and walks towards the next goal.
  • Both sisters are playable. Some puzzles need the small one.
  • Every puzzle has undo, try again and a hint.
  • Saves itself in every room.
