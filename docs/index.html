The stories

Series 1 has 16 stories, from autumn to the next summer, held together by grandpa's childhood club, Klub Hvězdička. Stories 1–14 are ready as comics and as games. Click one to look inside.

Made for small hands

  • One click does itNo verb menus. Big things to click, with a hand cursor.
  • Nothing scaryNo way to lose, no timers. Every "culprit" is an animal or a kind person.
  • Stuck? Click the dogJoey sniffs and walks towards the next thing to do.
  • Two detectivesSwitch between the sisters. Some puzzles need the small one.
  • Czech and EnglishBig hand-lettered subtitles, so a grown-up can read along.
  • Saves by itselfEvery room is saved; "Pokračovat" goes on where you stopped.
